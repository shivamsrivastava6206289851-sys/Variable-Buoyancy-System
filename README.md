**Project Name:** Variable Buoyancy System (VBS) for Underwater Vehicles

Designed and developed a Variable Buoyancy System (VBS) prototype for controlled depth and pitch regulation in underwater vehicles through variable ballast volume.

- **CAD Design & Prototyping:** Designed system components in SolidWorks and 3D-printed the CAD models to fabricate the physical prototype.
- **System Integration:** Developed a prototype incorporating two ballast tanks, two pumps, and four servo-operated valves for water transfer and buoyancy control.
- **Mathematical Modeling:** Formulated governing equations incorporating buoyancy, gravity, hydrodynamic drag, and ballast volume dynamics.
- **Dynamic Analysis:** Simulated depth-control behavior, sinking dynamics, and terminal velocity using MATLAB.
- **Control System Analysis:** Investigated the effects of pump flow rates and valve operation on the system's dynamic response.

**Tools & Technologies:** SolidWorks, 3D Printing, MATLAB, Dynamic System Modeling, Prototyping
